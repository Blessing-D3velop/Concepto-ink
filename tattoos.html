CONCEPTO INK AFTERCARE

Take care. Keep your art.

Your tattoo is a fresh piece of artwork that needs proper care while it heals. Follow these steps to protect it.

01

Keep It Clean

Gently wash your tattoo with clean hands and mild, fragrance-free soap. Do not scrub the tattoo or use rough cloths.

02

Moisturise

Once clean and dry, apply a thin layer of the recommended tattoo aftercare moisturiser. Avoid applying too much product.

03

Don't Pick

Peeling, dryness and itching can happen during healing. Do not scratch, pick or pull at flakes or scabs.

04

Avoid Direct Sun

Keep your healing tattoo away from direct sunlight. Once fully healed, use a suitable sunscreen to help protect the artwork.

05

Avoid Soaking

Avoid swimming, baths, hot tubs and prolonged soaking while your tattoo is healing.

06

Wear Loose Clothing

Avoid tight clothing that constantly rubs against your tattoo. Keep the area comfortable and allow it to breathe.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How much does a tattoo cost at Concepto Ink in Johannesburg?

Tattoos are priced by size: tiny (up to 2×2 cm) R350–R450, small R550–R750, medium R900–R1,300, large R1,500–R2,200 and extra-large or detailed pieces from R2,500. Sessions are also available: hourly R1,000–R1,200, 3-hour R2,800–R3,300, half day R3,500–R4,000 and full day R5,500–R6,500. Final quotes are confirmed at consultation.

Which tattoo styles do you offer?

Fine line (R600), black & grey (R700), realism (R1,200), portraits (R1,500), script lettering (R500), minimalist (R450), ornamental (R800), African / spiritual / symbolic work (from R800) and fully custom designs (R2,000–R2,500).

Do you do cover-ups and touch-ups?

Yes. Cover-ups start with a consultation, old tattoo touch-ups are R500, tattoo modifications are R700 and major redesigns are R150–R300.

How do I book a tattoo, and what is the booking fee?

Use the booking page. A R200 booking fee is paid securely through PayFast to confirm your slot and is deducted from your final price. You can also WhatsApp us on 083 531 9734.

How should I look after a new tattoo?

Wash gently with clean hands and mild, fragrance-free soap, then apply a thin layer of aftercare moisturiser once clean and dry. Don't scratch or pick, keep it out of direct sun, avoid swimming, baths and hot tubs while it heals, and wear loose clothing over it.

Can I get a refund on a tattoo?

Tattoos are custom artwork, so refunds are generally not available if you change your mind after the work is done. If you have a concern about quality or execution, contact us so it can be assessed. See our refund and exchange policy for how deposits, cancellations and rescheduling are handled.
