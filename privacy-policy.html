Privacy Policy

YOUR PRIVACY

Your information, handled with care.

Concepto Ink respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

01
WHO WE ARE

Who We Are

Concepto Ink, 48 1st Street, Orange Grove (Fairwood), Johannesburg, 2192, is the responsible party for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us on 083 531 9734 (call or WhatsApp).

02
WHAT WE COLLECT

Information We Collect

  • Your name and surname, email address and phone/WhatsApp number.
  • The service you want to book and any notes you give us, such as preferred dates, placement or reference ideas.
  • A payment reference when you pay the R200 booking fee. PayFast processes the payment; we do not receive or store your card details.
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, phone or social media.
  • Basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) that is processed by our website host and by third-party services used on the site.
03
WHY

How We Use Your Information

  • To arrange, confirm and manage your appointment.
  • To process your R200 booking fee.
  • To contact you about your booking, rescheduling and aftercare.
  • To answer your enquiries.
  • To meet legal and record-keeping obligations and to keep this website working properly.
04
PAYMENTS

Payments

Booking fees are paid through PayFast's secure checkout. Your card or bank details are entered on PayFast and are handled under PayFast's own privacy and security practices.

05
SHARING

Who We Share It With

We only share personal information with the service providers we need to run the website and the business: PayFast (payments), Vercel (website hosting), Google (fonts and the embedded map) and Cloudflare (icon files), and Meta's WhatsApp when you message us. We do not sell your personal information.

06
COOKIES

Cookies & Third-Party Content

We do not use advertising cookies, and this website does not currently run analytics tools. Embedded third-party content, such as the Google Map on our contact page, may set its own cookies under that provider's policies. If we add analytics or advertising in future, we will update this policy.

07
RETENTION

How Long We Keep It

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, including any legal, tax or record-keeping requirements.

08
SECURITY

Keeping It Safe

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your personal information. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09
YOUR RIGHTS

Your Rights Under POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) you may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, and you may object to its processing or withdraw your consent where it applies. Contact us on 083 531 9734 and we will respond as soon as reasonably possible.

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you have the right to complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

10
CHANGES

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page, with the date shown below, is the one that applies.

NEED HELP?

Questions about our policies?

Message or call us and we will gladly explain anything before you book.
