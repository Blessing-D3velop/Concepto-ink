BEFORE YOU BOOK

The agreement between you and us.

These Terms & Conditions apply to everything you do with Concepto Ink: visiting this website, booking an appointment, paying the booking fee and receiving a service at our studio. By booking or paying, you confirm that you have read and accepted them.

01
WHO WE ARE

About Concepto Ink

Concepto Ink is a private tattoo, piercing and beauty studio at 48 1st Street, Orange Grove (Fairwood), Johannesburg, 2192. You can call or WhatsApp us on 083 531 9734.

Studio hours: Monday to Saturday 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM and Sunday 10:00 AM – 5:30 PM.

02
SERVICES

Services & Pricing

We offer custom tattoos, ear, face and body piercing, and permanent beauty treatments such as microblading, ombré powder brows, lip blush, brow lamination and lash services.

Prices on this website are our current listed prices. Tattoos are quoted by size, detail and placement, and the final price is confirmed at consultation. Prices may change from time to time, and we will confirm the price of your service before work begins.

03
BOOKING FEE

Bookings & the R200 Booking Fee

  • Bookings made through this website require a R200 booking fee, paid online through PayFast.
  • The booking fee secures your appointment and is deducted from the final price of your service.
  • The fee is for the booking only — it does not cover the full cost of your service. The balance is payable at the studio on the day.
  • We aim to confirm your date and time with you on WhatsApp within 24 hours of receiving your booking fee.

How deposits are treated if you cancel, do not attend or reschedule is set out in our Refund & Exchange Policy.

04
NOTICE

Cancellations & Rescheduling

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule a tattoo or beauty appointment. Late cancellations, repeated rescheduling and no-shows may result in the loss of the booking deposit and/or require a new deposit before another appointment can be booked.

If we need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, we will contact you as soon as reasonably possible and offer an alternative appointment or refund the applicable deposit where appropriate.

05
YOU

Your Responsibilities

  • Give us accurate contact details and booking information.
  • Tell us before your appointment about anything that may affect your service, such as allergies, medical conditions or medication.
  • Follow the aftercare instructions we give you.
06
RESULTS

Tattoo, Piercing & Beauty Services

Tattoos and permanent beauty treatments are personalised services. Healing and results vary from person to person, and healing-related changes do not automatically constitute a defect in the service.

Beauty treatments cannot be exchanged once they have been performed. Where a concern is raised, we will assess it and, where appropriate, may offer a correction or touch-up, subject to the treatment type, the healing process and professional assessment. Where a follow-up or perfecting session is listed with a service, it is included as described.

07
AFTERCARE

Aftercare

You are responsible for following the aftercare instructions provided by Concepto Ink. Problems resulting from improper aftercare — such as picking or scratching, contamination, excessive sun exposure or swimming during the healing period — may affect eligibility for a complimentary correction or touch-up.
