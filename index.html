Frequently asked questions

Where is Concepto Ink located?

Concepto Ink is a private studio at 48 1st Street, Orange Grove (Fairwood), Johannesburg, 2192. See directions and contact details.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM, and Sunday 10:00 AM – 5:30 PM.

What services does Concepto Ink offer?

How do I book an appointment, and what is the booking fee?

Use the booking page. A R200 booking fee is paid securely through PayFast to confirm your slot and is deducted from your final price. You can also WhatsApp us on 083 531 9734.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

Please give at least 48 hours' notice. Late cancellations and no-shows may forfeit the booking deposit. See our refund and exchange policy for how deposits, cancellations and rescheduling are handled.
