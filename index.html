ABOUT CONCEPTO INK

WHERE ART MEETS IDENTITY

Concepto Ink is all about taking layering to the next level with tattoos, body piercing, body painting and modifying the body in artistic outlets. Whether conceptually designed or simplified, tattoos will instantly kick start your season.

Every piece begins with a conversation. From the first sketch to the final line, work is created in a private Johannesburg studio where the focus stays entirely on you and the artwork.
