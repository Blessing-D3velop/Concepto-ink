GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How much do microblading, lip blush and lash services cost?

Brows: tint R150, shape R180, shape + tint R250, lamination + tint R500, lamination + shape + tint R550. PMU brows: microblading R1,800, ombré powder brows R2,000, blade & shade R2,200, nano brows R2,200 and combo brows R2,300. Lips: lip blush R2,200, lip neutralisation R2,300 and lip blush + liner effect R2,400. Lashes: tint R150, lift R450 and lift + tint R550.

What is the difference between microblading, ombré powder brows and combo brows?

Microblading creates fine, hair-like strokes; ombré powder brows give a soft, shaded, powdered look; combo brows blend both techniques. Every appointment begins with a conversation about the look you want.

Is a follow-up session included with PMU treatments?

Yes — a 6–8 week perfecting session is included with PMU brow treatments.

How do I book a beauty treatment, and what is the booking fee?

Use the booking page. A R200 booking fee is paid securely through PayFast to confirm your slot and is deducted from your final price. You can also WhatsApp us on 083 531 9734.

What if I'm not happy with a beauty treatment?

Beauty treatments are personalised, so they can't be exchanged once performed. Contact us as soon as possible so we can assess the matter; a correction or touch-up may be offered depending on the treatment type, healing and professional assessment. See our refund and exchange policy for how deposits, cancellations and rescheduling are handled.
