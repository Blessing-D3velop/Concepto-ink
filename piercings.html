GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How much does a piercing cost at Concepto Ink?

Ear piercings start at R250 (single lobe), with upper lobe R300, helix R350, tragus, conch, flat and forward helix R400, daith, rook and snug R450 and industrial R550. Face piercings: nostril R400, eyebrow R450, and septum, high nostril, lip, labret, vertical labret and medusa R500. Body piercings: navel R500, nipple R500 single or R900 pair, and tongue R600.

Which piercings can I get?

Ear piercings (lobes, helix, tragus, conch, daith, rook, snug, industrial), face piercings (nostril, septum, eyebrow, lip and labret) and body piercings (navel, nipple and tongue). See our gallery for examples.

How do I book a piercing, and what is the booking fee?

Use the booking page. A R200 booking fee is paid securely through PayFast to confirm your slot and is deducted from your final price. You can also WhatsApp us on 083 531 9734.

Where is the piercing studio and when are you open?

48 1st Street, Orange Grove (Fairwood), Johannesburg, 2192. Open Monday to Saturday 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM and Sunday 10:00 AM – 5:30 PM. Get directions.

Is the booking fee refundable?

The R200 booking fee secures your appointment and is deducted from your final price. See our refund and exchange policy for how deposits, cancellations and rescheduling are handled.
