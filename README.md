# Concepto Ink – Tattoo & Beauty Studio Website

🌐 **Live Website:** https://concepto-ink.com/

**Client Project | Designed & Developed by Blessing Muthivhi**

## About the Project

Concepto Ink is a website I developed for a tattoo and beauty studio to give the business a professional online presence and make it easier for customers to explore its services and book appointments.

The idea behind the project was to create more than just a basic business website. I wanted to build an experience where visitors could browse tattoo designs, explore beauty treatments, view pricing, learn about the studio and complete their booking process online.

The website has a modern black and gold design, with a separate dusty rose theme for the beauty section. It is fully responsive and includes interactive elements throughout the different pages.

## Features & Functionality

### 1. Multi-page Website

The website is divided into dedicated pages, making it easier for visitors to find the information they need.

- **Home:** Introduction to the studio and its services.
- **Tattoos:** Tattoo services, styles, pricing and aftercare information.
- **Beauty:** Beauty treatments, service categories and pricing.
- **Gallery:** A combined tattoo and beauty gallery.
- **About:** Information about the studio.
- **Contact:** Business contact information.
- **Booking:** Online appointment booking with payment integration.

### 2. Interactive Service & Pricing System

Instead of displaying long price lists directly on the page, I implemented interactive pricing cards.

Visitors can:
- Browse different service categories.
- Open pricing information through popups.
- View individual services and their prices.
- Select a service and proceed directly to the booking page.
- Have their selected service automatically populated in the booking form.

### 3. Online Booking & PayFast Integration

One of the main features of this project is the online booking system.

I integrated **PayFast** to allow customers to pay a R200 booking fee when scheduling an appointment.

The booking functionality includes:

- Appointment booking form.
- Service selection.
- Customer information collection.
- R200 booking fee.
- PayFast payment redirection.
- Booking success page.
- Payment cancellation handling.
- WhatsApp fallback for customers who prefer direct communication.

This allows customers to move from browsing services to making a booking without having to arrange everything manually.

### 4. Interactive Gallery

The gallery brings together tattoo and beauty work in one place.

Features include:
- Category filtering (All, Tattoos and Beauty).
- Image previews.
- Fullscreen lightbox.
- Responsive image layouts.

### 5. Animations & User Experience

I added several animations to make the website feel more interactive and engaging.

These include:
- Scroll-triggered reveal animations.
- Fade-up and zoom-in effects.
- Image animations.
- Animated hero backgrounds.
- Hover effects on cards.
- Interactive navigation.
- Smooth transitions between elements.

### 6. Two Visual Themes

The website uses two different visual styles while maintaining a consistent overall design.

- **Tattoo section:** Black and gold aesthetic.
- **Beauty section:** Dusty rose pink accents with a softer appearance.

The themes share the same overall layout but have different colour accents depending on the section.

### 7. Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

I also implemented SEO improvements to help the business establish a stronger online presence.

These include:

- Individual page titles and meta descriptions.
- Canonical URLs.
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata.
- Structured data (JSON-LD).
- XML sitemap.
- Robots.txt.
- Search engine verification support.
- Descriptive page content.
- Custom 404 page.
- Redirects and security headers.

### 8. Legal & Business Information

The website also includes dedicated pages for important business policies:

- Policies.
- Refund & Exchange Policy.
- Terms & Conditions.
- Privacy Policy.

These help customers understand the studio's policies before making a booking.
